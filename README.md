# Farmhouse Invasion

A SMAPI mod for Stardew Valley that spawns a wave of monsters around your farmhouse every morning.

## What it does

Each day when you wake up, a random group of monsters (5 to 10) will be waiting for you outside your farmhouse. The monster type is random too: you might face Bugs, Flies, Duggies, Grubs, Green Slimes, or Rock Crabs.

Monsters only spawn on free tiles around the farmhouse, so you can still block the invasion.

## Notes

- In multiplayer, only the host spawns the monsters.
- Monsters persist until you defeat them.
